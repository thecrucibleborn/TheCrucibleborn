The Crucibleborn

A fantasy adventure comic series by Zachary Hutchinson

✦ ·͙*̩̩̥͙˚̩̥̩̥✦ Comic currently in development ✦˚̩̥̩̥*̩̩̥͙·͙ ✦

Presumed dead by her convent, Sister Aim (a sky nun who has never touched the ground) is sent into the ancient Cruciblehold City ruins to dispose of her newborn, a child condemned by the Reverend Mother for its blasphemous and unheard of abilities. But right as she prepares to leave the infant to its demise, it is rescued by two outcast kids from a separatist faction with the power to control life and death itself. Torn between her vows and a sudden, reluctant instinct to protect the child, Sister Aim abandons her Order forever. The four quickly form a found family, bound by choice as much as necessity and flee into the Great Wastes for a safe place to live as themselves, but survival in the Wastes is brutal. They aren't just being hunted by a rival faction that commands weather and water, intent on dissecting and weaponizing the baby, but are also being tracked by an underground rebel with ties to the child's existence that no one thought possible.

The Story

The world is divided by blood and ability.

THE LOAM live simple lives, yet grow life from the dust. THE AERETH float above the world with disdain. THE IGNATTI forge civilizations of steel and steam. THE BRAKKEN colonize with their command of weather and water.

For centuries, these factions have kept their distance, enforcing a strict separation between their different kinds of supernatural abilities. They have also kept a secret.

Sister Aim was supposed to be a vessel for the Aereth's future. She was supposed to give birth to a new acolyte for the Order of the Aether. Instead, she gives birth to Toll. Toll is the firstborn to possess the abilities of both the Loam and the Aereth. Something thought to be a biological impossibility, or worse, the world's oldest sin. The Order brands the infant a blasphemy and commands Aim to leave the baby in a temple in the ancient Cruciblehold City ruins.

She descends to the ruins prepared to do her duty. But before she can fly away, two young Loam siblings stumble upon her in the dark. Confronted by the living in a place for the dead, she finds she cannot go through with it. Curiosity overcomes Sister Aim and her obedience to the Order begins to fissure.

She befriends the Loam siblings who discovered her, Pemo and Pik, who are hiding a sin of their own. She has also been caught in the gaze of Scarlet, an exiled Ignatti woman who has spent sixteen years building a resistance in the shadows.

Now Aim is walking for the first time in her life. Pursued by a vengeful exile, the war's rogue marauders, and a Colonel who wants to dissect the impossible, Aim and her new friends protect the child who proves the world's history is a lie.

The factions erased a people once to keep their power, and they will do anything to do it again.

The Creator

Zachary Hutchinson (she/her, b. 1991, Wyoming, MI) is a Chicago-based artist and educator whose interdisciplinary practice spans video, sculpture, performance, and comics. She holds a BFA in Studio Art from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and an MFA in Moving Image from the University of Illinois Chicago, and she currently teaches at both.

After years making work for galleries and theaters, she has returned to tell the stories that made her want to be an artist and to work in the medium that made her want to be an artist: comics! She is excited to share her first long-form narrative comic series, The Crucibleborn, which deals with themes close to her heart, such as chosen family, bodily autonomy, and the refusal of complicity. In this new work Hutchinson approaches comics as a space where mythic world-building and emotional intimacy can coexist, creating narratives that are both accessible and formally rigorous.

She lives in Chicago with her three cats, Mary, Nancy (aka Tutu), and Chacha. When not drawing, writing, or teaching, she can usually be found thrifting or sniffing perfume.
