The Story

In a world where people are strictly segregated by their special abilities, a sheltered sky-nun, forced to the ground for the first time in her life, must learn to walk across a hostile wasteland to save her hybrid infant from a military scientist determined to dissect it and a calculating exile determined to use it to rewrite history.

The world is divided by blood and ability.

THE LOAM live simple lives, yet grow life from the dust. THE AERETH float above the world with disdain. THE IGNATTI forge civilizations of steel and steam. THE BRAKKEN colonize with their command of weather and water.

For centuries, these four factions have kept their distance, maintained their borders, and enforced a strict separation of powers. They have also kept a secret.

Sister Aim was supposed to be a vessel for the Aereth's future. Instead, she gives birth to Toll. This child possesses the abilities of both the Loam and the Aereth, a biological impossibility that threatens to unravel the world's political order. The Order brands the infant a blasphemy and commands Aim to leave the baby in a temple in the ancient Cruciblehold City ruins.

She descends to the ruins prepared to do her duty. But before she can fly away, two young Loam siblings stumble upon her in the dark. Confronted by the living in a place for the dead, she finds she cannot go through with it. Aim's obedience to the Order shatters.

She falls in with the Loam siblings who discovered her, Pight and Pik, who are hiding from a terrifying past. She also finds herself caught in the gaze of Scarlet, an exiled Ignatti woman who has spent sixteen years building a resistance in the shadows.

Now Aim is walking for the first time in her life. Pursued by a vengeful exile, the war's rogue marauders, and a Colonel who wants to dissect the impossible, Aim and her new friends protect the child who proves the world's history is a lie.

The factions erased a people once to keep their power. They will do anything to do it again.

The Creator

Zachary Hutchinson (she/her, b. 1991, Wyoming City, MI) is a Chicago-based artist and educator whose interdisciplinary practice spans video, sculpture, performance, and comics. She holds a BFA in Studio Art from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and an MFA in Moving Image from the University of Illinois Chicago, and she currently teaches at both.

After years making work for galleries and theaters, she has returned to tell the stories that made her want to be an artist and to work in the medium that made her want to be an artist: comics! She is excited to share her first long-form narrative comic series, The Crucibleborn, which deals with themes close to her heart, such as chosen family, bodily autonomy, and the refusal of complicity. In this new work Hutchinson approaches comics as a space where mythic world-building and emotional intimacy can coexist, creating narratives that are both accessible and formally rigorous.

She lives in Chicago with her three cats, Mary, Nancy (aka Tutu), and Chacha. When not drawing, writing, or teaching, she can usually be found thrifting or sniffing perfume.
