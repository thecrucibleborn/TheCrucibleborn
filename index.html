About

There are four peoples in the world, and none of them talk about the fifth.

The Aereth hover above the clouds in a convent of white stone, shaping matter with their hands and discarding any child born without the gift for it. The Loam grow crops out of dead desert and have built an entire religion around never finding out how far their own power actually goes. The Brakken run a naval empire on storm and lightning, where the powerless operate the pumps that keep the capital from drowning and are told their suffering is holy preparation. The Ignatti live in a mountain republic of iron and combustion and are very proud of how progressive they are, right up until you ask a Nullie or an immigrant how it's going.

Sister Aim is eighteen years old. She has never touched the ground, never disobeyed anyone, and never been permitted to want anything. When she gives birth to a child with impossible abilities (the Order's levitation and the Loam's ability to give and take life) the Reverend Mother orders her to leave the infant in the ruins below to die. Aim does as she is told. She carries the baby down. Then she picks it up and walks out of every life she has ever known.

Two lost Loam siblings find her in the temple: Pight, thirteen and already tired, and Pik, ten and hiding something under their hat. Further off, an exiled pacifist with white hair, an Aereth boy who was never supposed to survive his own infancy, and a woman named Scarlet who has spent sixteen years looking for exactly this child are all watching and following.

The walk ahead is across a dead desert, into a city kept dry by people nobody will admit exist, and toward a resistance that has been quietly gathering for half a lifetime.

The Crucibleborn is a comic about inherited lies, the families you build when the one that made you tries to kill you, and a baby who should not exist.
